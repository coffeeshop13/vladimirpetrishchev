# Editorial brief

Write in English for thoughtful operators, data leaders, and product people who are making AI useful inside real organisations.

The voice is clear, calm, specific, and practical. Prefer a strong point of view, concrete examples, and useful trade-offs over generic inspiration. Use first person only when describing a general working perspective; do not invent personal employers, clients, metrics, anecdotes, or credentials beyond what is already public on the website.

Each article should be 800–1,200 words, have a useful title, a one-sentence description, and 3–5 short tags. Use Markdown headings, paragraphs, and lists. Avoid hype, fabricated citations, unverified current events, and investment, medical, or legal advice.

Suggested themes:

- Designing AI products that survive contact with business constraints
- What data leaders should operationalise before scaling an LLM feature
- The organisational design of effective AI and analytics teams
- Evaluation, observability, and trust for production AI systems
- Choosing between RAG, fine-tuning, workflow automation, and simpler software
